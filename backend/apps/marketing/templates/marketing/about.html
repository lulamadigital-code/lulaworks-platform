About us

We build software for the people who build things

LulaWorks is a modern operations platform built for contractors, engineering firms, mining contractors, maintenance teams, industrial service providers and construction businesses — anywhere in the world.

Why we built LulaWorks

Contracting is hard enough without the admin. Most contractors run their business across spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, email chains and a few disconnected apps. A quote lives in one place, the purchase order in another, the site photos on a phone, and the invoice in a third system entirely. Information falls through the cracks, margins leak, and no one ever has the full picture of a job in one place.

We built LulaWorks to fix exactly that — to take a contractor from the first customer enquiry all the way to the final payment on one intelligent platform, with AI doing the tedious data entry along the way.

Our mission

To give every contractor — from a two-person team to a fifty-person operation — the kind of operating system that used to be reserved for large enterprises: connected, intelligent, and simple enough to actually use on a busy site.

Our vision

A world where running a contracting business is about doing great work, not fighting paperwork — where quoting, buying, delivering and getting paid all flow together, and the numbers are always clear.

Built around real problems

  • Quote faster and win more work, without re-typing the same details.
  • Know the true cost and profit of every job as it happens.
  • Buy materials at the best price, with a supplier history that builds itself.
  • Keep the field team accountable with GPS-stamped check-ins.
  • Get paid on time with clean, compliant invoices and clear tracking.
