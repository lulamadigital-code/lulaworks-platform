Privacy Policy

Effective date: {% now "j F Y" %}

LulaWorks ("LulaWorks", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have. We handle personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa, and apply the principles of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create a company or a user account we collect your name, email address, phone number, company details and password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form).

Usage information

We collect information about how you use the platform — pages visited, actions taken, device and browser type, and IP address — to operate, secure and improve the service.

Uploaded documents

When you upload documents (such as RFQs, purchase orders, supplier invoices and delivery notes), we store and process them so we can provide the features you use, including AI extraction.

Cookies

We use cookies as described in our Cookie Policy.

2. How we process your data

We process personal information to: provide and maintain the service; authenticate users; process your documents and generate outputs; handle billing and subscriptions; provide support; and comply with legal obligations. Our lawful bases include performance of our contract with you, your consent (where required), our legitimate interests in operating the service, and legal compliance.

3. Third-party services

We use trusted third-party providers for hosting, storage, payment processing and — where you enable AI features — AI processing. These providers process data only on our instructions and under appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. AI processing

Where you use AI features, the relevant document text may be sent to an AI provider to extract or generate content. This happens only when you trigger an AI action. We do not use your data to train third-party public models, and the deterministic (non-AI) parts of the platform never send your data to an AI provider.

5. Data security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, role-based access controls, audit logging and strict tenant isolation so one company can never access another's data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. After account closure we retain data only as required to comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access, correct, update or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to request a copy of your data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. International transfers

Where data is processed outside South Africa, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place consistent with POPIA and, where relevant, GDPR.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the service or by email, and the effective date above will be updated.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email privacy@lulaworks.co.za.
