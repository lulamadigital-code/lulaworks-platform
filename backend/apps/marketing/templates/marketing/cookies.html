This Cookie Policy explains how Lulaworks uses cookies and similar technologies when you use our website and platform.

Essential cookies

These are required for the platform to work — they enable core functions like page navigation, form submission and security. The Service cannot function properly without them, so they cannot be switched off.

Authentication cookies

These keep you securely logged in and maintain your session as you move between pages. They are cleared when you log out or your session expires.

Preference cookies

These remember choices you make — such as your selected billing cycle or display preferences — so the experience is consistent each time you return.

Analytics cookies

Where enabled, these help us understand how the website and platform are used — which pages are visited and how features perform — so we can improve them. Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

How to disable cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or remove cookies and notify you when a site sets one. Note that disabling essential or authentication cookies will prevent you from logging in and using the platform.
